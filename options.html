SPOT THE POD

No account required.

How it works

Spot the Pod searches a public podcast directory using your language preference and browser locale. Episode audio comes from each podcast publisher.

You do not need to create an account or have any preexisting subscription.

Privacy

Your language and episode-length preferences are saved locally. Search terms and catalogue requests are sent to Apple’s public Search API. Audio is streamed directly from podcast publishers. Spot the Pod has no analytics or application server.
